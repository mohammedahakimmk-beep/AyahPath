Privacy Policy

AyahPath — Learn the Qur'an, at your own pace.

Version 1.0 · Effective: September 1, 2026

At AyahPath, your privacy and trust matter deeply to us. This Privacy Policy explains in clear and simple language what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. Our design goal is privacy-first: your recitation is analyzed entirely on your device, and we collect only the minimum amount of personal data needed to operate the Service. This Policy applies to all users of the Service, and AyahPath welcomes users of all ages.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how AyahPath collects, uses, discloses, retains, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services. We are committed to protecting the privacy of every user, including users who are minors, and to being open and honest about our data practices. This Policy applies to all users, whether adults or children of any age. Throughout this Policy, "personal information" means information that relates to you and can be used to identify you.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information needed to operate the Service: (a) account credentials, namely the email address you use to register and either a password (hashed and stored securely by our authentication provider) or the identity information provided by your chosen sign-in provider (such as Google); and (b) the learning data you generate, including your learner profile, your progress, your completed lessons, your mastery scores, and your memorization records. We do not collect your name, phone number, physical address, or precise location unless you choose to provide them. We do not sell your personal information, and we have never sold personal information.

3. On-Device Recitation Analysis & Audio

When you use the recitation practice feature, the Service captures audio from your device's microphone to analyze your recitation. This audio is processed by an AI model that runs entirely on your device, and it is never uploaded to, transmitted to, or stored on our servers or on any third party's servers. Because recitation audio never leaves your device, your recitation and voice are fully private and are not part of your online data.

4. Online Account Data & Storage

To provide an online, synchronized experience and to keep your progress safe, we store certain data associated with your account on secure servers via Google Firebase Realtime Database. This includes your learner profile, learning progress, completed lessons, mastery scores, and memorization records. Your learning data is tied to your private account and is never shared with other users, other applications, or third parties for advertising or any commercial purpose. Access is protected by Firebase security rules so only you can read or modify your own records.

5. Authentication & Sign-In Data

Account creation and sign-in are handled by Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google. When you sign in with an email and password, that data is processed by Firebase, which securely hashes and stores your credentials. When you sign in with Google, Google shares only the minimum identity information needed to authenticate you. We never receive or store your raw password on our own servers. Your login session is stored locally on your device.

6. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to operate and improve the Service, personalize your learning plan, keep your data synchronized, authenticate you and protect your account, respond to your questions, and comply with applicable law. We do not use your personal information for targeted advertising, and we do not build profiles of you for unrelated purposes. We may use aggregated, de-identified data for analytics and product improvement.

7. Children's & All-Ages Privacy

AyahPath welcomes users of all ages, including children. We have designed the Service to be appropriate and safe for users of all ages, and we apply our privacy protections to every user regardless of age. We do not collect more information from children than from any other user, and we never use personal information to target advertising at children. The Service contains no advertising, no marketing tracking, and no public-facing social features that would expose a child's information. We encourage parents and guardians to review this Policy and our Terms of Service together with their children.

8. Protecting Your Data & Security

We take the security of your information seriously and use reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational measures to protect it, including secure, encrypted transmission and access controls. No method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information. You also play a role: you are responsible for keeping your password and login credentials private and secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain your account data while your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service and to comply with our legal obligations. You may delete your learning data, or your entire account, at any time from within the Service. When you delete your data or account, we take reasonable steps to remove your personal information; some copies may remain briefly for technical, legal, or security reasons.

10. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, update, or delete your personal information; to restrict or object to certain processing; to data portability; and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. You can often exercise these rights directly within the Service. We will respond to verifiable requests promptly and within required timeframes. You may withdraw your consent at any time by ceasing to use the Service and deleting your data and account.

11. Sharing & Disclosure of Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing or advertising purposes. We may share information only with service providers who help operate the Service (such as Firebase), to comply with legal obligations, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a merger or transfer of our business.

12. Third-Party Services & Links

The Service relies on certain third-party services, most notably Google Firebase and Google Sign-In, which have their own privacy policies. Where we rely on a third-party service, we share only the minimum information necessary for that service to function. The Service or our website may contain links to other sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of any third-party sites, and this Policy does not apply to them.

13. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored on servers located outside the country in which you reside, including data centers operated by our service providers such as Google Firebase. By using the Service you consent to such transfers. Where required by law, we will take steps to ensure that suitable safeguards are in place for such transfers.

14. Cookies, Analytics, & Tracking

The mobile application itself does not use cookies, and we do not engage in the kind of cross-site or cross-app tracking used to build advertising profiles. Our website may use basic analytics to understand general, anonymous traffic patterns, but we do not use advertising cookies or track users for advertising purposes.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will notify you of material changes by updating the effective date and, where appropriate, providing notice within the Service. Your continued use of the Service after any changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

16. Contact Us

If you have any questions, comments, or concerns about this Privacy Policy, our data practices, or your personal information, please contact us through the support contact details provided on our website. We take your privacy questions seriously and will respond as promptly as we can.
